4 On your iPhone: pair them & make the sticker

  1. Get both files onto the phone, together. Easiest: open this page in Safari on the iPhone and download both files right there. On a Mac or PC, AirDrop both files to the iPhone in one go. The two files must keep the same name — they're a matched pair.
  2. Save both to Photos. In the Files app, select both files, tap Share, then “Save to Photos” (save both, not just one). iOS spots the shared pairing ID and merges them into a single Live Photo — you'll only see one item in Photos.
  3. Check it plays. Open it in Photos and long-press — it should animate, with the LIVE badge up top. If the two files landed separately, delete both and re-save them together.
  4. Turn it into an iMessage sticker. In Photos, long-press the subject in the Live Photo and tap “Add Sticker” — or in Messages, open the + menu → Stickers → + and pick the Live Photo. Live Photo stickers stay animated in the chat.

Why two files?

A Live Photo isn't one file — it's a JPEG + a short MOV linked by an identical content ID stamped into both files' metadata. That stamp is the whole trick the paid apps charge for. This page writes it for free, right in your browser.

Good to know

Re-encoded clips are silent (stickers don't need sound, and it keeps the MOV tiny). For the sharpest result, use the “keep original” option with MP4/MOV clips. If your browser can only record WebM, you'll get a warning — switch to Safari or Chrome for a pairable .MOV.
